# Page Flipper

A web app for iPad that turns your printed sheet music into full-screen pages you can flip hands-free.

**Step 1 (this version):** photograph each page, straighten it (optional cleanup removes shadows and colored marks), save pages as a piece, and play it full screen. Turn pages by tapping (left third = back, anywhere else = forward), swiping, or with a Bluetooth page-turner pedal (arrow / Page Up / Page Down keys).

Teach mode: tap 🎹 Teach turns, record the last bar of each page once, and tap Test to check it is recognized.

Auto-turn: in the player, the app listens for the taught last bar of the page on screen and turns the page. Tap the top of the screen for controls: turn on/off, and how early to turn (on the last note, ½ s, 1 s or 2 s early). If it turns by mistake, tap the left side to go back; it waits a few seconds before listening again.

Everything is stored on the device in the browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded.

## Use on iPad
Open the site in Chrome, then Share › Add to Home Screen so it opens full screen like an app.
